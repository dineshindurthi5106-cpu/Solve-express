ℹ️ Municipal Severity Matrix Guide

🟢 Severity 1-2 (Green): Routine / Low Priority

Cosmetic requests, faint road markings, dirty signage, routine uncollected trash, standard potholes. Target resolution: 24 - 48 Hours.

🟡 Severity 3-4 (Yellow): Elevated / Urgent Hazard

Broken water mains, fallen live power lines, overflowing sewage entering homes, dengue epidemic hazard, main transit blockages. Target resolution: 1 - 8 Hours.

🔴 Severity 5 (Red): Critical / Life-Threatening

Gas explosions, active armed shootings, unconscious/severely burned casualties, electrocution in flooded zones, structural collapses. Target emergency dispatch: 15 Minutes.
